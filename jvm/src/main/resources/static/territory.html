🌾 Wheat: 0
💰 Gold: 0
⚡ Income: 0.0 wheat/s
👑 Abdications: 0

How to Play

  • Click empty tiles to build wheat fields (costs wheat)
  • Click wheat fields to level them up (costs wheat)
  • Higher levels produce more wheat per second
  • After building your first wheat field, you can also build farms
  • Farms boost nearby wheat fields by 25% per farm level
  • Fill all tiles to unlock abdication
  • Abdicate to earn gold and reset the field
  • Use gold to unlock more tiles
